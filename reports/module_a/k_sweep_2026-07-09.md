## Constrained outcome (disclosed amendment)

Adopting k=4 would rewrite the public 6-segment taxonomy consumed by
`schema_contracts/`, Module B ingestion, the dashboard, and the closed
F-039/F-051 invariants — a product/governance decision beyond this issue.
A taxonomy-stability constraint (k=6) was added to the rule as a disclosed,
post-sweep amendment; **constrained selection: k=6** (ARI 0.5418,
silhouette 0.2618). The k=4 signal stands recorded here for maintainer
review: revisiting the taxonomy would recover ~0.05 ARI and merge the two
pairs of profile-similar clusters the k=6 solution splits.
